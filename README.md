  <h1>Chocolate Fudge</h1>
  <img src="chocolate-fudge.jpg" alt="a plate of rich chocolate fudge square">
  <h2>Description</h2>
  <p>Chocolate fudge is a rich, sweet dessert made from sugar, butter, and milk or cream. It melts in your mouth and offers a deep, chocolate flavor.</p>
  <p>This simple recipe takes only a few minutes to prep and makes a wonderful treat for parties, holidays, or any sweet craving.</p>
  <h2>Ingredients</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>3 cups chocalate chips</li>
    <li>1 can sweet condensed milk</li>
    <li>1 tablespoon of butter</li>
    <li>2 tablespoons of sugar</li>
    <li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Steps</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>line pan with parchment paper</li>
    <li>combine all ingredients together</li>
    <li>stir constantly until everything mixes</li>
    <li>Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla extract. Pour into the prepared pan and chill until firm.</li>
    </ol>
